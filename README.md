# A Material a Day

Every day, one compound from the convex hull of thermodynamic stability of the
[Alexandria database](https://alexandria.icams.rub.de/): what it is, whether it is
known, how one would make it, and what it could be for.

## What an entry contains

Identity and structure, computed properties, an assessment of stability, synthesis
routes (direct, metathesis, and open-system where needed) with a firing window and the
impurities to expect, possible or present applications, a section on how the entry
could be wrong, and the literature. The crystal structure is included as a CIF.

## How to read the numbers

- All energies are density-functional calculations at 0 K. A reaction that is downhill
  is not necessarily fast, and nothing here accounts for temperature.
- Stability margins of a few tens of meV/atom are within the error of the method. Each
  entry quotes the margin beside the error measured for that class of compound.
- "No report found" means exactly that. It is not a claim of novelty.
- Synthesis sections are proposals derived from thermodynamics. They are not tested
  procedures and carry no safety assessment beyond the hazards named.
